Research Experience

Jan 2025 — Present

Undergraduate Researcher

Faculty Research Lab · Texas A&M University

Independently designed and built a multimodal rigid/flex PCB in Altium Designer integrating temperature, humidity, acceleration, vertical force, and shear detection, driven by a Nordic nRF5340 (BLE) microcontroller module. Built a proof-of-concept shear-warning device using FSRs detecting ~5 N shear for as little as $20. Designed validation experiments generating Bland-Altman plots and built Python analysis tools; work being prepared for IEEE journal submission.

Jun 2025 — Aug 2025

Research Experience for Undergraduates (REU)

HAND Engineering Research Center · Texas A&M University

Built the actuation and control system for a backdrivable, n+1 underactuated, four-DOF tendon-driven robotic finger with a four-bar coupling. Implemented PID and feedforward control across four DC motors with external magnetic encoders, using motor torques and Jacobians to drive commanded positions. Designed and fabricated motor mounts and wired/soldered the full electronics system.
